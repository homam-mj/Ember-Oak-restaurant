Our Story

Slow food, soulful fire

Ember & Oak was born from a simple belief: that the best meals are made over open flame, with patience and care. Our kitchen runs entirely on wood-fired ovens and charcoal grills, coaxing deep flavour from every ingredient.

Every dish on our menu changes with the seasons. We work directly with local farms across Turkey to bring the freshest produce, finest meats, and most extraordinary wines to your table.
